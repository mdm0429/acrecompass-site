Common Questions from Farmers

Which lender funds the fastest?
National Funding and Fora Financial typically fund in 1–3 business days. FSA direct loans take 30–60 days due to government processing.
What’s the maximum FSA farm loan amount?
FSA Direct Loans cap at $600,000. FSA Guaranteed Loans — where a commercial lender makes the loan and USDA backs it — go up to $2.24 million.
Do farm lenders pull my credit?
Most commercial lenders run a soft pull during pre-qualification. A hard inquiry happens only when you submit a full application. FSA uses its own creditworthiness criteria, not a FICO score.
Can I get a farm loan with bad credit?
Yes. FSA Beginning Farmer loans and USDA microloans are designed for borrowers who cannot get conventional credit. Lendio also works with lower credit profiles through its 75+ lender marketplace.
What’s the difference between an operating loan and a farm ownership loan?
Operating loans cover annual expenses — seeds, fertilizer, labor — and typically run 1–7 years. Ownership loans finance land purchases and run 20–40 years at lower rates.
