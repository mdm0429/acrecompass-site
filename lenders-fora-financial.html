Overview

Fora Financial is the right choice when you need more than $500K in fast financing — the ceiling at which National Funding stops and Fora Financial begins to differentiate.

With a maximum of $1.5M for working capital and up to $750K for equipment, Fora serves mid-size agricultural operations that have outgrown smaller fintech lenders but don't want to wait 30–60 days for Farm Credit underwriting.

Pros

  • Highest non-bank loan limits — up to $1.5M in working capital
  • Dedicated funding advisor assigned to larger applications
  • Minimum credit score 570 — one of the most accessible options
  • Funding in 24–72 hours with streamlined documentation
  • Equipment financing up to $750K with terms to 60 months

Cons

  • Wide rate range (7–35% APR (annual percentage rate)) — actual rate depends heavily on credit profile
  • Short working capital terms (4–18 months) increase monthly payments
  • No SBA loan products available through Fora
  • Factor-rate pricing can obscure true annual cost
  • No agricultural specialization — general business lender serving farms

Who This Is For

Large-scale operations needing $500K+ in working capital quickly — especially livestock feedlots, large grain operations, or farms with complex ownership structures where traditional banks move too slowly.

Who Should Look Elsewhere

Smaller operations under $200K should compare National Funding (lower minimums) or Lendio (marketplace competition). Any farmer who can wait 30+ days will save significantly with FSA (Farm Service Agency) Direct.

The 570 minimum credit score is the lowest of any lender we reviewed, making Fora accessible to operations with imperfect credit histories.

Equipment loan terms extending to 60 months (5 years) are meaningfully longer than most fast-funding lenders, reducing monthly payment pressure on major machinery purchases.

What Fora Offers

  • Working Capital Loans — up to $1.5M, terms 4–18 months, for pre-planting expenses, payroll, and operating needs
  • Equipment Financing — up to $750K, terms up to 60 months, for tractors, combines, and other farm machinery
  • Revenue-Based Financing — flexible repayment tied to monthly revenue, suited to operations with seasonal cash flow

Rate Structure

Equipment financing uses standard amortized loan pricing — straightforward APR. Working capital loans use factor rates for some products; Fora discloses the factor rate upfront, but you must convert to APR for meaningful comparison against Farm Credit or FSA rates.

Application Process

The Fora application is primarily online and takes 10–15 minutes. Unlike National Funding's strictly self-serve flow, Fora typically assigns a dedicated funding advisor to larger applications.

You'll need 3–6 months of bank statements, basic business documentation, and a credit authorization.

  1. Submit the online application at forafinancial.com
  2. A funding advisor contacts you within 24 hours for larger requests
  3. Upload 3–6 months of business bank statements
  4. Receive an offer within 24–48 hours
  5. Review APR/factor rate and total cost before accepting
  6. Funds in your account within 72 hours of approval

Best Use Cases for Farmers

Fora Financial is strongest when the loan amount exceeds $500K or when you need equipment financing for a larger purchase with terms beyond 24 months. Ideal candidates:

  • Operations needing $500K–$1.5M in fast-access capital
  • Farmers buying machinery in the $200K–$750K range who want 5-year terms
  • Operations with credit scores between 570 and 640 who have been turned down elsewhere
  • Farms with at least $150K in annual revenue and 6+ months in business

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Fora Financial's minimum credit score?
Fora Financial works with credit scores as low as 570, making them one of the more accessible options for farm operators with imperfect credit. However, rates improve significantly with higher scores — borrowers with 650+ will see substantially better terms. Fora considers your business revenue and time in operation alongside credit score.
How fast does Fora Financial fund loans?
Fora Financial can fund approved loans in as little as 24–72 hours after receiving complete documentation. Their online application takes about 10 minutes, and you'll typically receive a preliminary decision within a few hours. Final funding speed depends on documentation verification and loan amount.
What is factor rate pricing?
Instead of traditional APR, Fora Financial uses factor rates (e.g., 1.15–1.45). To calculate total repayment, multiply the loan amount by the factor rate. A $100,000 loan at a 1.20 factor rate means you repay $120,000 total. This translates to roughly 15–35% APR depending on the term length. Factor rates are common in short-term business lending.
Can I use Fora Financial for a land purchase?
No — Fora Financial is not suitable for farmland purchases. They specialize in working capital loans (up to 24 months) and equipment financing (up to 5 years). For land purchases, you need FSA Direct loans, Farm Credit, or a commercial agricultural bank that offers 20–30 year real estate terms.
Does Fora Financial require collateral?
For working capital loans, Fora Financial typically does not require specific collateral — they use a general business lien (UCC filing). For equipment financing, the equipment itself serves as collateral. This makes Fora accessible for farmers who don't want to pledge farmland or other assets for short-term capital needs.
What documentation does Fora Financial require?
Fora Financial requires 3–6 months of bank statements, a valid ID, and proof of business ownership. They do not require tax returns, business plans, or the extensive documentation that FSA and Farm Credit need. This streamlined approach is why they can fund faster, though it comes at a higher cost.
