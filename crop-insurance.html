Quick Recap

  • Federal crop insurance covers 490 million acres and over 100 crops in the U.S.
  • Revenue Protection (RP) is the most popular policy type — it covers both price drops and yield losses
  • USDA subsidizes 38–67% of your premium depending on the coverage level you choose
  • Whole-Farm Revenue Protection (WFRP) covers your entire operation under one policy — best for diversified farms
  • You must buy crop insurance through a private agent; sales closing dates vary by crop and region
