California Agricultural Loan Market: What to Know in 2026

California farmland at $12,000 per acre average is the most expensive in the country — and premium vineyard land in Napa Valley can reach $200,000+ per acre. This fundamentally changes the financing math compared to the Corn Belt. The standard FSA Direct loan limit of $600,000 covers less than one acre of prime wine country land, making the FSA Guaranteed Loan Program (up to $2.24 million) the more relevant government-backed option for most California farmers.

Who This Is For

California farmers and specialty crop growers seeking financing — including the CDFA Farm Loan Guarantee (up to 85% coverage), American AgCredit (Farm Credit), and FSA programs. Covers California-specific considerations for orchard, vineyard, and irrigation financing.

Who Should Look Elsewhere

Farmers outside California should use their state-specific guide. California's CDFA guarantees and American AgCredit's service territory are state/region-specific.

Specialty crop underwriting is a distinct discipline. California lenders look at long-term enterprise budgets, water rights, and crop-specific risk profiles rather than grain yield history. Almonds, pistachios, wine grapes, and strawberries all have different cost structures, revenue cycles, and production risks — and lenders experienced with California agriculture underwrite each differently. American AgCredit (the Farm Credit institution serving California, Hawaii, Nevada, New Mexico, Alaska, Wyoming, Colorado, and Oklahoma) is the primary specialist lender for California agriculture.

California-Specific Underwriting Considerations

Water rights and irrigation infrastructure materially affect land values and lender willingness on California farmland purchases. Lenders typically require water rights documentation and sometimes an independent water supply analysis before committing to a land loan. For specialty crop operations, multi-year enterprise budgets showing cash flow projections through crop establishment are standard underwriting requirements.

California Ag Loan Application Checklist

Gather these documents before contacting lenders to speed up the underwriting process.

  • 3 years federal tax returns (Schedule F for farm income)
  • Current balance sheet (assets, liabilities, net worth)
  • Multi-year enterprise budget (especially for specialty crop purchases)
  • Legal description, appraisal, and water rights documentation (if land purchase)
  • Crop production and sales history (contracts, marketing agreements)
  • Government-issued ID and Social Security number for credit pull
  • Existing loan statements (equipment notes, operating lines, land mortgages)
