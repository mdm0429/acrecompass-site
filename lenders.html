How We Rate Agricultural Lenders

Every lender on this page was evaluated by the AcreCompass research team across five categories. Lenders are ranked on editorial merit — no lender can purchase a higher ranking. FSA Direct is included without compensation because it is the best rate available to qualified farmers.

Rates & Fees
30% weight
Rate transparency, actual cost ranges, origination fees, and total cost comparison vs. FSA benchmark.
Loan Products
25% weight
Types offered, flexibility, ag-specific features, and whether products match how farms actually borrow.
Ease of Application
20% weight
Online application quality, documentation requirements, and funding speed from application to money in account.
Customer Service
15% weight
Agricultural expertise, responsiveness, and quality of farmer reviews from third-party sources.
Transparency
10% weight
Public rate disclosure, eligibility clarity, and absence of hidden fees or confusing pricing structures.

For the full methodology, see How We Rate Lenders.
