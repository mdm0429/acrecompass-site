How the Lendio Marketplace Works

Lendio is not a lender. It is a loan marketplace — you submit a single application, and Lendio's technology matches you with relevant offers from its network of 75+ partner lenders.

The lenders you may be matched with include commercial banks, credit unions, SBA-approved lenders, and alternative fintech lenders.

Pros

  • Access to 75+ lenders through a single application — widest comparison network
  • SBA 7(a) loan access that most single-lender platforms cannot offer
  • Minimum credit score 560 — the most accessible option reviewed
  • Soft pull prequalification protects your credit during comparison shopping
  • Loan amounts up to $5M across all product types

Cons

  • Not a direct lender — you deal with the matched lender for servicing
  • Rate and term vary by matched partner, making upfront comparison harder
  • Application process takes 2–7 days (slower than direct online lenders)
  • Less control over which lender you end up with
  • No agricultural-specific products or expertise in the platform itself

Who This Is For

Farmers with credit scores in the 560–650 range who want to compare multiple offers before committing. Also ideal for operations seeking SBA 7(a) loans or farmers who have been declined elsewhere and want to cast a wide net.

Who Should Look Elsewhere

Farmers with 680+ credit should go directly to Farm Credit for the best rates. Operations needing same-day funding should apply directly at National Funding — the Lendio matching process adds 2–7 days.

The process works like this:

  1. Complete Lendio's online application (approximately 15 minutes)
  2. Lendio performs a soft credit pull and reviews your business financials
  3. Within 24 hours, you receive a set of matched offers from compatible lenders
  4. You review and compare offers — rates, terms, and fees vary by lender
  5. You select your preferred offer and complete that lender's additional paperwork
  6. Funding timeline depends on the matched lender (typically 2–7 business days)

The core trade-off: you gain access to many lenders at once, but you give up direct control over which lenders receive your application. Expect calls from multiple lenders after submission.

What Types of Loans Are Available Through Lendio

Lendio's marketplace covers a broader range of loan types than any single direct lender we reviewed:

  • SBA 7(a) loans — up to $5M, typically the lowest non-FSA (Farm Service Agency) rates for qualified borrowers
  • SBA 504 loans — for commercial real estate and major equipment (farmland may qualify)
  • Term loans — standard amortized loans from multiple lenders
  • Equipment financing — for machinery, vehicles, and farm equipment
  • Lines of credit — revolving credit for operational needs
  • Merchant cash advances — revenue-based financing for established operations

SBA loan access is Lendio's biggest differentiator over National Funding and Fora Financial. SBA 7(a) loans carry government-backed guarantees and typically offer rates significantly below most alternative lenders.

Lendio for Farmers: Pros and Caveats

Lendio works best for farms that have not yet identified the best direct lender and want to compare multiple offers without submitting multiple applications.

It is particularly well-suited to operations with credit scores between 560 and 650 that may not qualify for Farm Credit or low-rate FSA alternatives.

✓ Pros

  • Single application for 75+ lenders
  • Access to SBA loans (not available at NF or Fora)
  • Good for credit scores 560–640
  • May find better rates than going direct
  • No cost to use the marketplace

✗ Cons

  • Not a direct lender — expect multiple lender calls
  • Best rates still go to 680+ credit
  • Not ag-specialized — general business platform
  • Rates and terms vary by matched lender
  • Harder to build long-term lender relationship

Credit Score and Qualification

Lendio's 560 minimum credit score is the lowest of all lenders we reviewed. However, the quality of offers you receive scales directly with your credit score. Borrowers at 560–599 will likely see high-rate alternative lenders in their match set.

Borrowers at 640–680 may receive a mix of term loans and SBA options. Borrowers above 680 are better served by going directly to Farm Credit or an SBA-approved lender.

Lendio vs. Going Direct to Lenders

The value of Lendio depends on your situation. If you already know which lender fits your needs — Farm Credit for land, AgDirect for equipment, National Funding for speed — going direct is faster and avoids the multi-lender follow-up calls.

If you're unsure which lender type matches your loan request and credit profile, Lendio's matching service is worth the trade-off.
