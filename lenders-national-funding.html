Overview

National Funding is the fastest option among the lenders we reviewed for operating capital under $500K.

Same-day credit decisions and funding in as little as 24 hours make it the default choice when you need cash before planting season and cannot wait for a bank's underwriting cycle.

Pros

  • Funding in as little as 24 hours — fastest agricultural lender reviewed
  • Minimum credit score 600 — accessible to more farm operators
  • Soft pull prequalification does not affect your credit score
  • Working capital up to $500K without real estate collateral
  • BBB A+ rated with $4.5 billion+ funded since 2002

Cons

  • Higher rates (7–24% APR (annual percentage rate)) compared to FSA or Farm Credit
  • Revenue-based factor pricing can be expensive on smaller loans
  • Maximum $500K — not enough for large land purchases
  • Shorter terms (4–24 months for working capital) require faster repayment
  • No patronage dividend (a rebate Farm Credit associations pay back to borrower-members from annual profits)s or cooperative member benefits

Who This Is For

Farmers who need operating capital within days — not weeks — and cannot wait for FSA processing. Best for seasonal bridge financing, urgent input purchases, or operations with 600+ credit that need speed over rate.

Who Should Look Elsewhere

Operations that can wait 30–60 days should apply for FSA Direct loans at 5.25% instead. Established farmers with 680+ credit will get better long-term rates through Farm Credit.

Rates start at 7% for borrowers with strong credit and established revenue history. Less established credit histories push rates higher, up to 24%.

For context, FSA direct operating loans run at 4.75%–5.50% — but require 30–60 days and in-person service center appointments. National Funding fills the gap for farmers who need funds in days, not weeks.

What National Funding Offers

National Funding offers three core products relevant to agricultural businesses:

  • Working Capital Loans — unsecured term loans from $5K to $500K, ideal for pre-planting inputs, payroll, and operating expenses
  • Equipment Financing — secured loans for machinery and equipment, up to $500K, with terms extending to 60 months
  • Revenue-Based Financing — flexible repayment tied to monthly revenue, useful for operations with uneven cash flow

Rate and Fee Structure

Working capital loans use simple interest rates, not factor rates. Rates are disclosed as APR before you accept, which is better than many fintech lenders who bury costs in factor rate pricing. Equipment loans use traditional amortized pricing.

How the Application Works

The application is fully online and takes approximately 10 minutes. You will need basic business information, 3–6 months of bank statements, and a credit check authorization.

National Funding does a soft pull for prequalification — a hard pull only occurs when you accept an offer.

  1. Complete the online application at nationalfunding.com (10 minutes)
  2. Connect bank statements or upload PDF statements
  3. Receive a same-day decision for most applications
  4. Review your offer and APR before accepting
  5. Funds deposited in 24 hours after acceptance

Who National Funding Is Best For

National Funding is the right choice when speed and simplicity outweigh rate optimization. Ideal candidates:

  • Farmers needing pre-planting capital in days, not weeks
  • Operations with at least $250K in annual gross revenue
  • Borrowers with 600+ FICO scores and 6+ months in business
  • Farms needing equipment financing without the longer Farm Credit underwriting process
  • Growers who have been turned down for FSA or bank loans due to timing

Is National Funding Worth It for Farmers?

Yes — when timing is the constraint. If you need capital before planting and your FSA service center appointment is six weeks out, National Funding's 24-hour turnaround solves a real problem.

The cost premium over FSA (roughly 2–18 percentage points) is the price of speed and simplicity.

If rate is your only priority and you have time to wait, FSA Direct remains the lowest-cost option.

If you need more than $500K, Fora Financial or Farm Credit better fit the scale. But for fast operating capital under $500K, National Funding earns its #2 ranking.
