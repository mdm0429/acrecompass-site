Quick Recap

  • FSA Direct loans offer the lowest farm loan rates in the U.S. — set by the Treasury, not by banks
  • FSA Guaranteed loans let you borrow up to .24M through a commercial bank with 95% federal backing
  • Farm Storage Facility Loans finance grain bins and cold storage at ~2.50% with 15% down
  • Beginning farmers get priority funding, reduced down payments, and extended terms across all FSA programs
  • All FSA loans carry fixed rates for the life of the loan with no prepayment penalties
